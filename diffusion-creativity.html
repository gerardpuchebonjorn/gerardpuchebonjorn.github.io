Creativity in diffusion models

Diffusion models have always struck me as something quite curious. The main idea is:

Take an image and add a little bit of random noise to it, then a bit more, and keep going for many steps until nothing is left but noise. Then train a neural network to do the opposite: given a noisy image, remove a little of the noise. Once it has learned that, we can start from pure noise and apply it over and over, and step by step an image appears.

The first thing that came to mind is that if you do this, then why do new images arise? We should simply go back to the training ones. A recent paper from Google, “On the Interpolation Effect of Score Smoothing in Diffusion Models” by Zhengdao Chen (ICLR 2026, see also the blog post), went into creativity in diffusion models and I thought it was a good exercise to break it down.
