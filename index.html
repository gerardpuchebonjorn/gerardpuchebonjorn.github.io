to me, reasoning is playing, and I love to play. I play for the sake of it: to fulfil curiosity, stimulate intellectual creativity, and for the pleasure of finding things out.

I love to have fun with puzzles — intellectually curious, creative and challenging problems and ideas. I believe your mindset and values define what you build, not the other way around. hence what I define here is that, you can see my work elsewhere on the site.

I try to rigorously follow substance over prestige. I don't like formalities or little nuances meant to slow the world down. unapologetic science is the best kind of science: your argument is as good as your logic, not your credentials.

the prize of great work is the work itself — seeing you have done something great, the pleasure of finding things out, and the recognition that other people use it. not honours or grades or anything else. I pursue interesting problems with challenging and direct people. if you think you fit this personality, please reach out.

I was born and raised in a small coastal town southwest of barcelona. I natively speak catalan, spanish and english. I enjoy music, skiing and reading.

you can find a lot of thoughts and notes on learning in my notes section, I am not a really good professor but some of the resources I have used are so you might find that useful.
